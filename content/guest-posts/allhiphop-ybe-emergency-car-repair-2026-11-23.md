Emergency Car Repair In Prince George's County: Call YBE Auto Repair Center

Car trouble never picks a good time. The car stalls at a light on Central Avenue. It cranks and cranks but will not start in the morning. The temperature gauge climbs into the red on the way home. The brakes start grinding a day before you have to drive across the county. When the car you depend on stops working, you need someone who will pick up the phone and tell you what to do next.

If you ever need emergency car repair in Prince George's County, call YBE Auto Repair Center at (202) 455-3822. The emergency line is answered seven days a week, 9:00 AM to 12:00 AM (midnight).

Safety Comes First

If anyone is hurt, the car is blocking traffic, or you feel unsafe, call 911 first. On a busy road, stay in the car with your seat belt on and your hazards flashing. If the brakes failed or the car is smoking, do not keep driving it. Once everyone is safe, call YBE for the car.

What YBE Handles

Car will not start or stalled out. YBE finds out why, whether it is a dead battery, a starter, an alternator, a fuel or ignition problem, or something bigger.

Check engine and warning lights. A warning light tells you something is wrong but not what. YBE scans for codes and diagnoses the cause before any parts are ordered.

Brake problems. Grinding, squealing, a soft pedal or a car that pulls when you stop. YBE repairs pads, rotors and calipers.

Overheating. A climbing temperature gauge is a reason to pull over, not push through. YBE checks the cooling system and finds the leak or the failed part.

Transmission trouble. Slipping, hard shifts or a car that will not go into gear get diagnosed and repaired at the shop.

Battery, alternator, starter and electrical problems. YBE tracks down the cause instead of swapping parts until something works.

Flat tires. YBE helps with the flat and handles tire repair, replacement and alignment at the shop.

Where The Repair Happens

When the problem can be handled where the car sits, YBE sends mobile help, including jump starts, mobile battery replacement, mobile diagnostics and minor mobile repairs. When the car needs the shop, bring it to 8632 Edgeworth Dr in Capitol Heights, where diagnostics, brakes, transmission, electrical and starting problems, tires and alignment, and A/C and heating are all handled under one roof.

YBE does not tow. If the car needs to come off the road entirely, YBE will tell you that honestly when you call, so you can arrange a tow instead of waiting on help that will not solve the problem.

Diagnosis First, Then The Repair

Emergencies are when people get sold parts they do not need. YBE diagnoses first, gives you a straight answer on what is wrong and what it takes to fix, and quotes before any work starts. Only the work you approve gets done.

How To Get Help

Call (202) 455-3822. Calling is the fastest way. If you cannot talk, use the form on the YBE Emergency Car Repair page to send your name, number, location and what the car is doing by text or email, and the shop will get back to you.
