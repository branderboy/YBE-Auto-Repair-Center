Stuck On The Road In Prince George's County? Call YBE Auto Repair Center

The car will not start in the parking lot after work. A tire goes flat on Central Avenue. The keys are sitting on the driver's seat and the doors are locked. The gas light has been on longer than you meant to let it. None of these feel small when you are the one standing next to the car.

If you ever need emergency roadside help in Prince George's County, call YBE Auto Repair Center at (202) 455-3822. YBE has been sending mobile help to drivers around Capitol Heights, Washington, DC and Prince George's County for years, under the name YBE Mechanic On Wheelz. Most of those calls get handled where the vehicle sits, without moving the car.

Safety Comes First

If anyone is hurt, the car is blocking traffic, or you feel unsafe, call 911 first. If a child or a pet is locked in the car, call 911 right away. On a busy shoulder, stay in the car with your seat belt on and your hazards flashing. If you can move the car fully off the road before calling, do it. Once everyone is safe, call YBE for the car.

What YBE Handles Roadside

Dead battery. A jump start gets most cars going again. If the battery keeps dying, YBE can replace it on the spot with mobile battery replacement.

Flat tire. YBE helps with the flat and installs your spare so you can get moving.

Locked out. Vehicle lockout help gets you back into the car when the keys are inside.

Out of gas. Emergency fuel delivery brings fuel to the car so you can get back on the road.

Car will not start, or stopped and you do not know why. Mobile diagnostics checks the problem where the car is, and minor mobile repairs can often fix it there instead of moving the car.

If the problem turns out to be bigger than a roadside fix, the full shop is at 8632 Edgeworth Dr in Capitol Heights. That is where diagnostics, brakes, transmission, electrical and starting problems, tires and alignment, A/C and heating, and body and paint work all happen under one roof.

What YBE Does Not Do

YBE does not tow. If the car needs to come off the road entirely, YBE will tell you that honestly when you call, so you can arrange a tow instead of waiting on help that will not solve the problem.

What To Have Ready When You Call

Keep your hazard lights on and stay with the vehicle if it is safe. Have your keys, ID and registration ready. Note the cross street, the exit or a landmark so the team can find you. Keep your phone on so YBE can call you back.

If you cannot talk, you can send your location and what happened by text to (202) 455-3822 or through the request form on the YBE Emergency Roadside Help page. Calling is still the fastest way to get help.

Where YBE Sends Roadside Help

YBE is based in Capitol Heights and serves Capitol Heights, District Heights, Forestville, Hillcrest Heights, Landover, Lanham, Largo, New Carrollton, Hyattsville, Bowie and Upper Marlboro, plus Washington, DC and the rest of Prince George's County.

When To Call

The YBE phone is answered seven days a week, 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM, Saturday and Sunday included. Save (202) 455-3822 in your phone now, so you have it the day the car will not start.
