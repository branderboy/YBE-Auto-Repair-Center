One Shop For Paint And Mechanical Work: YBE Auto Repair Center Repaints Commercial And Private Vehicles In Capitol Heights

People judge a vehicle by its paint before they hear the engine. Faded color, peeling clear coat and long scratches say the vehicle has been neglected, even when it runs fine. For a business, that message rides along on every job site and delivery route. For a private owner, it is the difference between a car that still looks cared for and one that looks tired.

YBE Auto Repair Center in Capitol Heights, Maryland has been fixing cars since 2006. The shop does body and paint work in the same building as its mechanical repair, which means one drop off covers the finish and whatever is going on under the hood.

A work truck or a service van is a sign that drives. Customers see the paint before they read the logo, and a faded or rusting fleet vehicle tells them something about the work before the crew gets out.

YBE repaints vans, work trucks and company cars. Dents, scratches and damaged panels are repaired before the new coat goes on, so the finish comes out straight and even across the vehicle.

For Private Owners, Bring The Car Back

A private repaint usually starts one of three ways. The paint has faded or oxidized after years in the sun. A collision repair left one panel a shade off from the rest. Or the owner wants a new color and a finish that looks factory again.

YBE handles all three. The body and paint team works on bumpers, fenders and panels, dent and scratch repair, collision damage, paint preparation and refinishing. Drivers come in from Capitol Heights, District Heights, Landover, Forestville, Hyattsville, Bowie, Upper Marlboro and across Prince George's County and Washington, DC.

How A Repaint At YBE Works

1. Inspection. The panels, dents, scratches and old paint are looked over first, and the job is quoted per vehicle after that inspection.
2. Body repair. Dents, scratches and damaged panels are repaired or replaced before any paint goes on. Damage is never painted over.
3. Preparation. Sanding and surface prep so the new coat bonds and lays flat.
4. Paint and refinishing. The color goes on across the full vehicle or the repaired panels and is matched to the rest of the car.

While the car is in the shop, the same team can take care of the mechanical side. If the brakes have been squealing, the steering pulls, a warning light is on, or the A/C stopped blowing cold, mention it at drop off and it can be handled in the same visit.
