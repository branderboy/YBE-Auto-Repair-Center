# Project Brief: Rebuild the YBE Auto Repair Center Website Around Its Google Business Profile

## Project Goal

Update the existing YBE Auto Repair Center website using the current Miro plan and convert it into a true multi-page local-business website.

The website must mirror the company’s Google Business Profile, integrate the five-part local SEO framework, and create a clear path from Google search to phone call, text message, directions, or appointment request.

Do not turn this into a generic auto-repair template. Use YBE’s real services, categories, history, location, service areas, customer language, and competitive advantages.

Before changing anything, inspect the current website structure and Miro plan. Preserve any useful branding, content, images, functionality, and working integrations.

# Content Style

Write for everyday drivers, not mechanics.

The copy should be:

* Direct
* Reassuring
* Easy to understand
* Specific about symptoms and solutions
* Local without forced city-name repetition
* Helpful during stressful vehicle problems

Avoid:

* Corporate language
* Generic claims such as “best-in-class”
* Technical explanations customers do not need
* Keyword stuffing
* Repetitive AI-style introductions
* Fake urgency
* Unverified guarantees

Use customer language such as:

* “My car won’t start.”
* “The check-engine light came on.”
* “My brakes are grinding.”
* “My car is pulling to one side.”
* “The A/C is blowing warm.”
* “My car is overheating.”
* “I have a flat tire.”
* “I’m stranded and need help.”

# Appointment and Roadside Workflows

## Appointment Form

Include:

* Name
* Phone
* Email
* Vehicle year
* Vehicle make
* Vehicle model
* Service needed
* Description of the problem
* Preferred date
* Preferred contact method

Do not promise an appointment until YBE confirms it.

## Roadside-Assistance Form

Roadside service should prioritize calling or texting over a long form.

If a form is included, request:

* Name
* Mobile number
* Current location
* Vehicle year, make, and model
* Roadside problem
* Whether the vehicle is in a safe location
* Optional photo upload

Display a clear emergency safety note telling customers in dangerous locations to contact 911 or the appropriate emergency authority first.

Do not advertise towing unless YBE actually owns, operates, or directly provides towing service.
